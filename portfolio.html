How I invest

Different structures, one discipline

The structure depends on access, stage and information. The standards don’t change.

  • Positions taken directly in private companies where the opportunity, management team, valuation, and path to value creation can be assessed with sufficient conviction.

    Direct investing provides the greatest visibility into the company, but it also requires disciplined diligence, concentration limits, and patience through periods when progress is not linear.

  • SPVs provide access to companies and transactions that may not be available directly, including later-stage opportunities and allocations led by experienced investors.

    The quality of the underlying company is only one part of the decision. The vehicle’s fees, carried interest, information rights, governance, transfer restrictions, and alignment with the sponsor must also withstand scrutiny.

  • Fund investments provide diversified exposure to specialized managers, sectors, stages, and markets. I look beyond headline performance to the manager’s sourcing advantage, decision discipline, portfolio construction, reserves, ownership strategy, and ability to support companies after the initial investment.

  • Funds of funds can provide access to a broader set of managers, vintages, and opportunities, particularly where individual fund access would be difficult or inefficient.

    They must justify the additional layer of fees through superior selection, diversification, access, and portfolio construction.

  • Public-market positions provide liquidity and exposure to companies building the infrastructure, compute, platforms, and enabling technologies underlying long-term economic change.

    A strong company can still be a poor investment when expectations, valuation, and concentration leave no margin for error.

What changes with stage
  • Angel to pre-seedFounder quality, technical insight, the severity of the problem, and evidence that a customer cares.
  • Seed to Series ACommercial traction, repeatability, retention and unit economics.
  • Series BGrowth plus operating discipline and a credible path through future financing.

Geography shapes access and follow-on capital, but never excuses weak fundamentals. Early is not the same as right.

Investment doctrine

Conviction without suspension of disbelief

Built across market cycles, including dot-com lessons that did not come cheaply.

  • 01Technology must ultimately create value for a customer.
  • 02A strong company can still be a poor investment at the wrong price.
  • 03Evidence should be appropriate to the company’s stage.
  • 04Exceptional upside must justify early-stage risk.
  • 05Portfolio construction matters as much as individual selection.
  • 06Follow-on capital should confirm conviction, not rescue it.
  • 07No opportunity is so compelling that discipline should give way to FOMO.

The full framework stays proprietary. Its purpose is simple: repeatable decisions, and conviction kept separate from enthusiasm.

Investment enquiries

Specificity makes the first conversation useful

Well-defined opportunities from founders, fund managers, SPV sponsors and co-investors are welcome. Say what it is, and why it fits.

Founders and companies

  • What the company does and the problem it solves
  • Who the customer is and what evidence of demand exists
  • Current stage, traction and geographic market
  • Capital being raised, proposed terms and intended use of proceeds
  • Existing investors and the relevant decision timeline
  • A link to the company’s website and presentation

Funds and SPVs

  • Investment strategy, stage, sector and geography
  • Manager or sponsor background
  • Fund size or transaction allocation
  • Portfolio-construction and ownership objectives
  • Fees, carried interest and principal terms
  • Track record or relevant prior investments
  • Closing schedule and remaining allocation

Co-investment opportunities

  • The company or asset and the transaction sponsor
  • Round or financing structure and valuation
  • Available diligence and proposed allocation
  • Decision deadline
