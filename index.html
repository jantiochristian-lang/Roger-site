One Africa

Shared competitiveness, not fragmented ambition

Africa’s largest opportunities — trade, AI, infrastructure, energy, capital — cannot reach full potential inside fragmented national markets.

Fragmented markets produce

  • Fragmented companies
  • Duplicated infrastructure
  • Smaller pools of capital
  • Limited negotiating power

Connected markets create

  • Scale
  • Improved productivity
  • Investment that arrives
  • A path from national to global

Countries keep their identities and build shared capabilities where scale matters. Collective strength, translated into measurable economic advantage.
